Triple Chocolate Brownies

Description

If you are looking for the ultimate triple chocolate brownie recipe, then these rich, indulgent and delicious brownies are the ones to bake. Packed with triple chocolate chunks, and with the perfect balance of chocolate throughout, these brownies have the delicious fudgy texture every baker is after.

Whether you are baking for birthdays, afternoon treats, a family gathering, or simply trying to satisfy a chocolate craving, this recipe is the one you will turn to again and again.

Ingredients

  • 200 g dark chocolate
  • 200 g unsalted butter
  • 3 large eggs (or 4 medium)
  • 275 g caster sugar
  • 100 g plain flour
  • 50 g cocoa powder
  • 100 g white chocolate
  • 100 g milk chocolate
  • 100 g dark chocolate

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 180ºc/160ºc fan and line a 9″ square baking tray with parchment paper.
  2. Melt together the unsalted butter and dark chocolate in a heatproof bowl over a pan of boiling water, on the microwave for 1-2 minutes until melted.
  3. Leave to the side to cool to room temperature for 5-10 minutes.
  4. In a new bowl, whisk together the eggs & caster sugar for a few minutes until the colour has turned pale, is very mousse like, and is double the original volume.
  5. You will know its done because when you lift the whisk up out of the mix it should leave a trail for a couple of seconds before disappearing.
  6. When whisked, pour the cooled chocolate mix over eggs and fold together carefully.
  7. Once completely combined, add the cocoa powder and plain flour on top of the chocolate mix and then fold together again.
  8. Once combined well, fold through the chocolate chips and pour into the prepared tin.
  9. Bake in the oven for 25-30+ minutes until there is a small wobble in the middle.
  10. Once baked, leave to cool in the tin completely. The, set the the brownies in the fridge for 3+ hours for best results.
